DA OPS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Controller: Daniel Aguiar Motors (DA Motors), Harare, Zimbabwe · App package: zw.co.damotors.fuel

DA OPS is a private operational tool for DA Motors staff: fuel-tanker drivers, service-station supervisors, logistics, finance and management. It is not intended for the general public. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Who can use it

Access requires a username (or fuel-card number) and PIN issued by DA Motors. There is no self-registration. Driver accounts are bound to one handset; signing in from another phone requires a manager's approval.

What we collect

  • Account & identity: your name, staff/driver username or fuel-card number, role, your assigned truck, and a one-way hashed PIN (we never store your PIN in readable form).
  • Location — station check: when you raise a fuel request at a service station, your device location is read once, at that moment, to confirm you are at the station you selected.
  • Location — delivery route recording (drivers only): when you start a fuel delivery trip (by raising the trip's fuel request or confirming collection at the depot), the app records your position periodically, roughly every 30 seconds or 50 metres, until the last drop is offloaded and the trip completes. Recording continues while the phone is in your pocket or the app is in the background. While it runs, Android shows a persistent notification, "DA OPS — trip in progress", so you always know. Recording stops automatically when the trip completes or is cancelled, and you can stop it by ending the trip. No location is recorded outside an active delivery trip. The route is used to confirm deliveries, estimate arrival times for the receiving station, review stops and detours, and resolve disputes about fuel losses in transit.
  • Camera / photos: photographs you take of an odometer, a delivery note or a tank reading, as evidence for the transaction you are submitting.
  • Operational data you submit: fuel requests, approvals, deliveries, warehouse and site stock, pump readings, prices, sales, cash declarations and reconciliations, with timestamps.
  • Device & usage basics: device identifier (for the one-handset binding), app version, push-notification token, screens opened and actions taken in the app, and standard technical logs (request time, IP address) kept for security and audit.

Why we collect it

Solely to operate DA Motors' fuel and site operations: authorising and recording fuel, verifying presence at a station, tracking deliveries from depot to station, managing stock, cash and pricing, and producing management reporting. Records are kept as a tamper-evident, append-only ledger for audit and accountability. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

How it is shared

Data stays within DA Motors' own systems and is processed on a DA Motors-controlled server. Delivery routes are visible to DA Motors logistics, operations and management staff, and to the receiving station as an estimated arrival time. Service providers that process limited data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — routes traffic securely to our server.
  • Google / Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications using a device token.
  • Google Maps / OpenStreetMap — route distance and map display; trip coordinates may be sent to these services to compute a route.

We may disclose records where the law requires it.

Retention

Operational records, including delivery routes, are retained as business and audit records for as long as DA Motors requires them. Photographs are stored with their transaction. Usage logs are kept for security review. Your account is deactivated when you leave DA Motors; records you created remain in the ledger.

Your choices

  • You may decline the camera or location permission; some actions (a station fuel request, starting a delivery trip) then cannot be completed.
  • Route recording runs only during a delivery trip you started, and ends when the trip ends.
  • You may request correction of master data (for example your name) via your administrator, and you may ask what data we hold about you.
  • To deactivate an account or request deletion of personal data that is not part of the audit ledger, contact us at the address below.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). PINs are stored only as one-way hashes. Driver accounts are bound to a single handset. The record store is append-only and cryptographically chained so tampering is detectable. Access to the server is restricted to DA Motors systems staff.

Children

DA OPS is a workplace tool for DA Motors employees and contractors. It is not directed at, and must not be used by, anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes and show the new date at the top.
