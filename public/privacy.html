DA OPS — Privacy Policy

Daniel Aguiar Motors (Zimbabwe) · Effective 11 September 2026

Internal operations app

DA OPS is an operations app used by the staff, drivers and site teams of Daniel Aguiar Motors ("DA Motors", "we") to run fuel retail and haulage operations: fuel requests and approvals, deliveries, site submissions, cash handling and vehicle tracking. It is not a consumer product and is only available to people we have given a login to.

What the app collects

  • Identity and login. Your name, staff role, site or vehicle assignment, and a login PIN (stored only as a one-way hash). Optionally a Google account email if you sign in with Google, and a device identifier that binds a driver login to one phone.
  • Location. Only when you use a location-based feature: confirming you are at a fuelling station when raising a fuel request, and background trip tracking from a fuel request until the delivery is offloaded. Location is not collected outside those operations.
  • Photos. Photographs you take in the app of an odometer, a fuel tank, a delivery note or a deposit slip. These are attached to the record they evidence.
  • Operational records you enter. Fuel, delivery, dip, price, cash and shift data you submit as part of your job.
  • Notifications. A push-notification token so the app can alert you to items needing your action.

How it is used

Solely to operate, evidence and audit DA Motors' fuel and logistics operations — approving fuel, reconciling deliveries and cash, tracking trips, and notifying you of work that needs your action. Records are kept in an append-only ledger so that history cannot be silently altered.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is processed on DA Motors' own systems and with the service providers needed to run the app (hosting, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications). Location and map distances may be computed with Google Maps / OpenStreetMap routing services.

Retention

Operational records are retained for as long as they are needed for accounting, audit and regulatory purposes. Location pings for a trip are kept with that trip's record.

Your choices

You can decline location, camera or notification permissions in your phone's settings; the features that need them will then be unavailable. To correct your details or ask about your data, contact your DA OPS administrator at DA Motors.

Contact

Daniel Aguiar Motors, Harare, Zimbabwe — via your DA OPS administrator or the support contact published on the app's store listing.
